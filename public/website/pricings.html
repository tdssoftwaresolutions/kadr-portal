Pricing Questions
Pricing Ke Sawaal
Who pays — the person filing or both parties?Kaun pay karta hai — file karne wala ya dono?
+
By default, the filing party pays. Split billing (both parties pay half) is available on request before the case begins.
Default mein file karne wali party pay karti hai. Split billing — dono half-half — request pe available hai case shuru hone se pehle.
What if mediation fails — do I get a refund?Agar mediation fail ho — refund milega?
+
The fee covers the process, not the outcome. If mediation was conducted in good faith, no refund is issued. You receive a Non-Settlement Report.
Fee process ke liye hai, outcome ke liye nahi. Agar mediation good faith se hui, refund nahi milega. Non-Settlement Report milegi.

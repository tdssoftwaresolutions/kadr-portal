Section 1

Professional Integrity

  • Experts must provide services honestly, transparently, and in good faith, ensuring the best possible outcomes for all parties involved.
  • Experts must avoid any conduct that could discredit the Platform or the dispute resolution profession.
Section 2

Confidentiality

  • Experts must maintain the confidentiality of all information disclosed during the dispute resolution process, unless required by law to disclose such information.
  • Experts are prohibited from using confidential information for personal or professional gain.
Section 3

Impartiality and Neutrality

  • Experts must remain impartial and neutral in all dispute resolution processes.
  • Experts must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to the Platform and the parties involved immediately upon becoming aware of them.
Section 4

Competence and Professional Development

  • Experts must possess the necessary qualifications, certifications, and experience relevant to their role.
  • Experts must engage in continuous professional development to enhance their skills and stay updated on best practices in dispute resolution.
  • Experts must not undertake cases for which they lack the necessary expertise or capacity.
Section 5

Communication

  • Experts must communicate clearly, respectfully, and promptly with all parties involved.
  • Experts must avoid making false or misleading statements about their qualifications, expertise, or the services they provide.
Section 6

Adherence to Laws and Regulations

  • Experts must comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and ethical standards in the jurisdiction(s) where they operate.
  • Experts must respect the cultural and social norms of the parties involved in the dispute resolution process.
Section 7

Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct is strictly prohibited:

  • Soliciting or accepting gifts, favours, or other benefits that could influence impartiality or judgment.
  • Engaging in any discriminatory, harassing, or unethical behaviour towards Users or other Experts.
  • Using the Platform for any purpose other than providing dispute resolution services.
Section 8

Reporting Violations

  • Experts must report any violations of this Code of Conduct by themselves or others to the Platform promptly.
  • The Platform reserves the right to investigate and take appropriate action, including suspension or termination, for breaches of this Code.
Section 9

Enforcement

  • The Platform reserves the right to monitor and evaluate the performance of Experts to ensure compliance with this Code of Conduct.
  • Violation of this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, including but not limited to warnings, suspension, or permanent removal from the Platform.
Section 10

Acknowledgment

Our Shared Commitment

By agreeing to this Code of Conduct, you affirm your commitment to uphold the principles outlined herein and contribute positively to the reputation and effectiveness of KADR.live.
